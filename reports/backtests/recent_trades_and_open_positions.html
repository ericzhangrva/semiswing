Tier Conviction Levels:
  • CONSENSUS: Dual-engine alignment (both Baseline OLS trend significance $t_{\text{norm}} \ge 1.8$ and ML classifier agree; 1.0% risk).
  • HIGH: Standalone ML qualification with top-quintile residual momentum (1.0% risk).
  • MEDIUM: Baseline trend qualification or secondary ML score (0.75% conservative risk).
  • LOW: Lower-conviction meta-labeling score ($S_{i,t} < 0.85$; 0.50% risk or conservative bottom tier).
Shares & Position ($): Dynamically calculated via Volatility-Parity / Fixed-Fractional Risk Sizing:
$$\text{Shares} = \frac{\text{Portfolio Equity} \times \text{Base Risk} \times M_t \times S_{i,t}}{\text{Stop Distance}}$$
Each trade risks a fixed fraction of total portfolio equity at execution, so position dollar allocations and share counts vary according to individual stock ATR volatility, tier conviction, meta-labeling multiplier $S_{i,t}$, and compounding portfolio equity over time.
Partial Profit Taking (TP1) & Blended Returns: When Take Profit 1 (TP1) triggers, partial shares are sold and stop is moved to breakeven. Exit ($) reports the final runner share exit price, while Return (%) and R Multiple reflect the blended net performance across the entire position.
Win Prob: Platt-calibrated posterior probability $P(\text{Hits } +1.5\text{R target before Hard Stop})$ produced out-of-fold by shallow LightGBM trees. With a 1.5:1 reward-to-risk ratio, any probability $P > 40.0\%$ yields positive mathematical expectancy.
Sizing Scalar: Continuous meta-labeling position sizing multiplier $S_{i,t} \in [0.50, 1.50]$ (López de Prado framework):
$$S_{i,t} = 0.50 + \text{Rank}_{\text{OOF}}(\hat{p}) \in [0.50, 1.50]$$
Maps predicted win probability into historical out-of-fold percentile rank, scaling conviction dynamically between 0.50x and 1.50x without expanding total risk caps.
Top Model Drivers (SHAP Mathematical Definitions): Single-feature marginal contributions detailing model conviction:
  • sector_rs: 20-day relative strength of SMH vs SPY: $$\text{sector\_rs} = \Delta\%_{20d}(\text{SMH}) - \Delta\%_{20d}(\text{SPY})$$ Positive values confirm semiconductor sector leadership.

  • resid_mom_20: 20-day idiosyncratic residual momentum relative to SMH after stripping market beta.
  • rs_rank: Cross-sectional percentile rank ($[0.0, 1.0]$) of 20-day residual momentum across the active tradeable universe.

  • slope_t_norm: Adaptive window $L_t$ sample-size normalized OLS $t$-statistic measuring directional trend conviction: $$t_{\text{norm}} = \frac{t_{\text{OLS}}}{\sqrt{L_t / 20}}$$
  • slope_accel: Multi-scale slope acceleration: $$\text{slope\_accel} = \frac{\beta_{10d} - \beta_{40d}}{\sigma_{20}}$$
  • ema20_slope: 5-day slope of 20-day exponential moving average in ATR units: $$\text{ema20\_slope} = \frac{\frac{\text{EMA20}_t}{\text{EMA20}_{t-5}} - 1}{\text{ATR\%}_{14}}$$
