Shares & Position ($): Dynamically calculated via Volatility-Parity / Fixed-Fractional Risk Sizing: Shares = (Portfolio Equity × Base Risk × M_t × S_i,t) / Stop Distance. Each trade risks a fixed fraction of total portfolio equity at execution, so position dollar allocations and share counts vary according to individual stock ATR volatility, tier conviction, meta-labeling multiplier S_i,t, and compounding portfolio equity over time.
Partial Profit Taking (TP1) & Blended Returns: When Take Profit 1 (TP1) triggers, partial shares are sold and stop is moved to breakeven. Exit ($) reports the final runner share exit price, while Return (%) and R Multiple reflect the blended net performance across the entire position.
Win Prob: Platt-calibrated posterior probability P(Hits +1.5R target before Hard Stop) produced out-of-fold by shallow LightGBM trees. With a 1.5:1 reward-to-risk ratio, any probability P > 40.0% yields positive mathematical expectancy.
Sizing Scalar: Continuous meta-labeling position sizing multiplier S_i,t ∈ [0.50, 1.50] (López de Prado framework). Maps predicted win probability into historical out-of-fold percentile rank: S_i,t = 0.50 + Rank_OOF(p). Conviction scales position sizes dynamically between 0.50x and 1.50x without expanding total risk caps.
